Kakatiya Kala Thoranam

  • Kakatiya Kala Thoranam is a historical arch in the Warangal district, of the Indian state of Telangana

    •

  • The Warangal Fort has four ornamental gates which originally formed the gates to the destroyed great

    Shiva temple which are known as Kakatiya Kala Thoranam or Warangal Gates.

  • Opened: 1200

  • The Kakatiya Kala Thoranam was built by Ganapati Deva during the 12th century.

  • It is an arch that is extensively ornate, reflective of the four identical gates in the Warangal fort.

  • The Kakatiyan arch has been adopted and officially incorporated into the Emblem of Telangana after the state bifurcation.
