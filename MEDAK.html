Medak_Cathedral

  • Medak Cathedral at Medak in Telangana, India.

    •

  • It is one of the largest churches in India and has been the cathedral church of

    the Diocese of Medak of the Church of South India since 1947.

    •

  • Originally built by Charles Walker Posnett British Wesleyan Methodists, it was consecrated on 25 December 1924.

    •

  • Address: Cathedral Compound, Mission Compound, Mission Compound, Medak, Medak, Telangana 502110

    •

  • Diocese: Diocese of Medak

  • The biggest attraction of the Cathedral is its stained glass windows depicting different scenes from Christ's life


Medak_Fort

  • This enormous fort was built about 800 years back in Medak

    •

  • The fort demands one’s attention with its typical construction.

    •

  • The fort was built during the 12th century under the reign of the Kakatiya ruler, Pratapa Rudra

  • It was initially called ‘MethukuDurgam’ in Telugu.

    •

  • The Medak Fort is a huge structure that takes nestling on the top of a hill.

  • It served as a command post for the Kakatiyas and later for the Qutub Shahis.

    •

  • Medak fort has about 500 steps to climb.

    •

Wargal Saraswati Temple

  • Wargal Saraswati Temple, or Sri Vidya Saraswati Temple

    •

  • It is a Hindu temple located in Siddipet district in Telangana, India.

  • The deity of education in Hinduism is Goddess Saraswati.

  • It is among the few temples of Saraswati in Telangana.

    •

  • It is maintained by the Kanchi Shanker Mutt

    •

  • Address: 1-6-15, 1-6-15, Road to Sri Vidyasaraswathi Temple, Gaddi annaram, Wargal, Telangana 502279

    •

  • It was built by Sri Yamavaram Chandrashekhara Sharma.


Edupayala vana durga bhavani temple

  • This famous Hindu temple is open to the public & sits in a scenic, forested area alongside a river.

    •

  • Address: 253R+42V, Edupayala Rd, Nagsanpally, Telangana 502331

  • Built-in 12th Century, Edupayalu Vana Durga Bhavani temple is one of the most famous and

    powerful pilgrim destinations in the region, dedicated to Goddess Kanakadurga.

    •

  • This is a picturesque shrine located amidst the lush green forest and natural stone formations inside a den.

    •

  • 8 Kms from Medak District at Edupayala is the Edupayala Vana Durga Bhavani temple

    dedicated to the most powerful Goddess Kanakadurga.

    •

  • The temple becomes inaccessible during peak monsoon season as the approach submerges under water.
