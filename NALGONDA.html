Nagarjun Sagar Dam

  • Nagarjuna Sagar Dam is a masonry dam across the Krishna River at Nagarjuna Sagar

    •

  • Nagarjun Sagar Dam is constructed between Nalgonda District of Telangana and Guntur Districts of Andhra Pradesh.

    •

  • World's largest masonry dam protected with 26 gates measuring 14m in height and 13m in width

    •

  • Opened: 1967

  • The dam has a storage capacity of nearly 11,472 million cubic meters with an irrigation capacity for 10 acres of land.

  • Lal Bahadur Canal is the name of Nagarjuna Sagar dam left canal

    •

  • The Project was inaugurated on 10th December 1955 by Late Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru

    •

  • Nagarjuna Sagar Dam is 57 years old
