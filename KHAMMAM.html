  • Beside the Godavari River, the huge 17th-century Bhadrachalam (or Sita Ramachandraswamy) Temple

  • The complex is dedicated to Lord Rama and has many ornate carvings.

  • Other nearby temples include Abhaya Anjaneya Temple, with large painted statues of Hindu gods and goddesses.

  • Distance from Hyderabad: 325 kilometres (202 mi)

  • Elevation: 90 m (300 ft)
