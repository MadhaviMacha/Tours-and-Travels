ARTS AND CRAFTS

BANJARA NEEDLE CRAFT

Banjara needle craft is a vibrant, handmade embroidery tradition from the Banjara (Lambada) tribal community in India, known for its intricate designs, bold primary colors, and the use of mirrors, cowrie shells, and bells stitched directly onto the fabric. This nomadic art form uses patchwork and applique techniques to create richly embellished textiles that reflect the culture and lifestyle of the Banjaras.

BIDRI

Bidriware is a metal handicraft from the city of Bidar, Karnataka, known for its intricate inlay work using silver on a blackened zinc alloy base. This 14th-century art form is now practiced in several parts of India, including Hyderabad in Telangana
