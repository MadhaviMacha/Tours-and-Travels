Statue_of_Equality_(Ramanuja)

  • The Statue of Equality is a statue of the 11th-century Indian philosopher Ramanuja.

  • It is located on the premises of the Chinna Jeeyar Trust at Muchintal, Ranga Reddy district in the outskirts of Hyderabad.
